## Lead

**COP31 collapses in Antalya** — The climate conference in Antalya,
Turkiye, ended on Sunday with no agreement after more than two weeks
of talks, the longest COP ever and the first major breakdown since
COP15 in Copenhagen in 2009. The final "Antalya Pact" draft dropped
every mention of fossil fuels; the EU, Britain and island states
refused to back it. Only 142m dollars in new funding was pledged.
Talks chair Chris Bowen called the result very disappointing.
— CNN, Le Monde, The Times, NPR/WCMU, 22–23 Nov.

## War in Europe

**Burning ship off Finland** — The Russian cargo ship Sparta II, hit
by drones in the Baltic on Saturday, was still smouldering on Sunday
as Finnish, Estonian and Russian crews fought the fire together. All
16 crew were evacuated; towing began on Sunday. Finland said the war
was spilling over.
— Reuters, CNN, 22–23 Nov.

**14 missing after Kyiv strike** — A Russian drone hit a residential
block near Kyiv early on Sunday, killing one person and leaving 14
unaccounted for, President Zelenskiy said.
— Euronews, Investing.com, 23 Nov.
